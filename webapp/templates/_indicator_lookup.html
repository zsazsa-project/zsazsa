{# Indicators in rendered Markdown, looked up from a card that opens when the pointer rests on one: in the data collection's MISP servers, or for a CVE at Vulnerability-Lookup. What is marked is core/indicators.py's, handed over by indicator_lookup_config(). The answer opens inside the text, under the block the indicator is in. Include it on a page that shows reports or products for reading, and on a product's form for the preview of its Markdown fields, before the scripts that render them: _markdown_js.html marks what it renders once this is on the page, and the .report-md-body and .md-body the server rendered are marked when the page has loaded. Not on the manual entry, whose text is the article. An event's page sets lookup_event first (data_collection._lookup_event), so the answer marks that event and can be added to it as a report; a page that shows events one after another calls zsazsaLookupEvent(event). In the preview of a product's Markdown field the answer can be put into the text instead, holding only what the product's TLP allows. Text from MISP, a report or Vulnerability-Lookup goes in with textContent and DOM methods only. #}
