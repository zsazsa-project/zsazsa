The draft is written from the saved dataset, without AI, and replaces what the field holds once you confirm. Edit it afterwards. In order, it says:
  1. The period, how many events the report is built from, from which sources with a count each, the events' dates, and how many were queued but left out at triage.

  6. Where AI was used: the sections drafted with "Draft with AI", saved or not, and the AI reading of imported reports.
