Writing a strong GIR

A General Intelligence Requirement captures an ongoing monitoring need rather than a one-time question. It should:

  1. Define a monitoring area. Describe the threat landscape, sector, or actor group to track continuously.
  2. Anchor it to stakeholders. Identify who benefits from this standing collection.
  3. Set a review cadence. GIRs should be revisited periodically to confirm they remain relevant.
  4. Plan the deliverable. Format and distribution should match the stakeholders' workflow.
  5. Bound the scope. Geography, sectors, threat actors, threat types and exclusions keep collection focused.
