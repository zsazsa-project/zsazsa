{# The "As it will appear" card of the PIR, GIR and RFI forms follows the form, as the stakeholder form's does. In the card, [data-preview-text] shows the value of the fields it names, [data-preview-choice] the badges of the ticked choices, and [data-preview-select] the chosen option, or the field in data-or-text when none is chosen. data-empty is what shows with nothing, in red when the field has data-required. #}
