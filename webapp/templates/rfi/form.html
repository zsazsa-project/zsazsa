Capturing a good RFI

An RFI is an ad-hoc question from a stakeholder. Capture enough context to triage and respond:

  1. Who is asking, and for what decision? The same question from a SOC lead vs. a CISO has a different answer.
  2. Tie it to a PIR or GIR if possible. Recurring RFIs hint at missing standing requirements.
  3. Set a realistic due date. SLA hints: High {{ sla_days.High }}BD, Medium {{ sla_days.Medium }}BD, Low {{ sla_days.Low }}BD.
  4. Always close the loop. Record the response and capture feedback to feed the metrics dashboard.
  5. Attachments and notes can be added from the RFI's own page once it is created (after you save).
